ShiftVault is a free, open-source ledger and shift tracker for Android that keeps working without a connection. No account, no sign-in, no advertising and no tracking: everything you enter stays in the app's local storage on your phone.

LEDGER
• Multiple accounts with running balances
• Income and expenses with categories and notes
• Every entry keeps its own currency, so mixed-currency accounts stay correct
• Search and filter by month, account, category or type

SHIFT TRACKER
• Log date, hours and earnings for every shift
• Monthly totals and an overview of what you have earned
• Store your hourly wage once and let ShiftVault calculate your pay

GOALS AND INSIGHTS
• Monthly savings targets with progress bars
• Per-account breakdowns
• See how many working hours are left until a goal is reached

CURRENCIES
• 12 currencies, formatted consistently in the interface
• Optional exchange-rate refresh from the Frankfurter API, with a local cache and bundled offline fallback rates

DESIGN
• OLED-friendly dark theme with true black and a light theme
• Six languages: English, German, Spanish, French, Italian and Portuguese
• Two launcher icons, switchable at any time
• Native system-bar theming, haptics and predictable back navigation

BACKUPS
• CSV export of the visible ledger for spreadsheets
• Complete JSON backup of accounts, entries, shifts, goals and settings
• Backups are written locally and never uploaded

PRIVACY
• No server, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDK
• The only outbound request is the optional exchange-rate refresh; if it fails, the app stays fully usable

ShiftVault is MIT licensed, contains no proprietary dependencies and is built from the public source at https://github.com/AeroBaden/ShiftVault
